Mpv∞ is a powerful and efficient Android video player built around libmpv, with an additional Native playback engine powered by AndroidX Media3. It combines broad format support, advanced playback controls, and a polished interface without advertisements or trackers.

Playback engines
- MPV engine: advanced libmpv playback, shaders, scripting, hardware and software decoding, and detailed playback controls.
- Native engine: AndroidX Media3 playback presented consistently as Native throughout the app.
- Automatic engine routing: Auto mode can use Native for compatible Dolby Vision items and HLS/DASH streams while keeping ordinary files on MPV.
- Manual engine switching: choose MPV or Native for the current video from the decoder sheet.
- Single-engine handoff: the inactive engine is stopped before the selected engine starts, reducing duplicated playback work and battery use.
- Fallback protection: when Native cannot render a video frame, Mpv∞ falls back to MPV without repeatedly retrying Native for the same item.
- Additional audio compatibility: Native playback includes the Media3 FFmpeg audio extension where supported by the device.

Player features
- Frame-by-frame navigation, chapters, A-B looping, custom skip segments, sleep timer, playback speed presets, and picture-in-picture.
- Gesture controls for seeking, volume, brightness, zoom, subtitle positioning, and subtitle scaling.
- Multiple audio and subtitle tracks, subtitle styling, online subtitle search, subtitle translation, and subtitle font management.
- HDR and video rendering controls, Anime4K upscaling, GPU debanding, ambient lighting, screenshots, and output statistics.
- Customizable portrait and landscape controls, themes, translucent player surfaces, and responsive tablet layouts.
- Local files, network streams, SMB, FTP, WebDAV, M3U playlists, yt-dlp streaming, torrent playback, and Google Cast.
- Music library, synchronized lyrics, visualizers, equalizer, audio filters, and background playback.
- Built-in scripting and configuration editors for Lua, JavaScript, mpv.conf, and input.conf.
- Thermal-aware shader and Anime4K safeguards plus low-power polling improvements.

## Replacement, relink, and debugging rights

For an LGPL-covered binary, replacement means: obtain the corresponding source at the pinned revision/archive, apply the repository's documented patches, rebuild with equivalent configure/Meson options, preserve the library's normal dynamic-link boundary, and ship the modified source and required notices with the app. Validate ABI/API compatibility and update the exact hash table. Do not replace an AAR by merely changing a URL or by relinking against an unknown binary.

LGPL reverse-engineering permissions for **debugging modifications** and the ability to modify and relink the covered library are intentionally preserved. In particular, do not strip away the application's ability to debug, replace, or relink LGPL MPV/FFmpeg/libplacebo/libbluray/libudfread components when distributing a modified app. The AAR verifier and MPVFlow checks are build safeguards, not legal guarantees. The notices alone are insufficient: the source, patches, build instructions, and any required relinkable application material still need to accompany a distribution as required by the applicable LGPL version.

## Distribution checklist and reproducibility limitations

* For each LGPL binary release, keep the corresponding source, local patches, build instructions, and any required relinkable application materials available to recipients under that component's terms. Source does not have to be embedded inside the AAR itself; use a compliant source-distribution method and verify the documented links and revision pins when publishing. The AAR and MPVFlow entry points above identify the repository's native build inputs.
* The helper repository's published layout differs from the app's renamed `.so` helpers, and no exact upstream commit or local rename/strip recipe is recorded here. This limits reproducibility; it does not establish that the upstream source was modified. Verify and retain the corresponding FFmpeg source and any actual modifications when distributing these LGPL helpers.
* The CPython Android libraries/zip are labelled `3.13.12`, but no complete source-to-bundle Android recipe is checked in. CPython's permissive PSF licence requires its notices and applicable attribution to be preserved; it does not impose LGPL-style corresponding-source or relinking obligations. A missing CPython build recipe is a reproducibility limitation, not by itself a PolyForm incompatibility.

This manifest is an acquisition and replacement guide—not a claim that every distributed binary is byte-for-byte reproducible or a legal certification of a particular release. The project's noncommercial restriction applies only to Covered Code; the independent third-party grants and recipients' rights under them remain intact.
